== LOSSES -- themes ==
Scars: what broke here, and the rule that stops it next time. Append only.


========================================================================
THE CHECKER CAUGHT THE GENERATOR                              2026-07-26
========================================================================
The first ramp put danger_darker at oklch lightness 0.45 on dark themes. It
looked right in a swatch strip and measured 2.25:1 against the surface -- a
warning colour nobody could see.

THE BAR WAS RIGHT AND THE MATHS WAS WRONG. The floor is 0.55 on dark now.

THE RULE: WHEN A CHECK FAILS, FIX THE THING IT CHECKED. Lowering the bar to
make a generator pass turns the checker into decoration, and the failure it
was built to catch ships anyway.


WATCH FOR:
  - RAMPS BELONG IN OKLCH. Mixing toward white in sRGB shifts hue and kills
    chroma -- a lightened blue comes back lilac and washed out.
  - CLAMP AFTER CONVERTING BACK. A colour can be valid in oklch and outside
    what a screen can show; a very saturated seed returns slightly tamer, and
    that is the conversion being honest rather than a bug.
  - PREVIEW MUST WRITE NOTHING. The form generates while you type; if preview
    wrote a row, every keystroke would be a theme.
